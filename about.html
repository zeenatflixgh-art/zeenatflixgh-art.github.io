About ZEENATFLIX GH

Welcome to ZEENATFLIX GH — Ghana's Home of Authentic African Stories.

ZEENATFLIX GH is a Ghanaian storytelling platform proudly based in Buipe, Savannah Region, Ghana. Founded in early 2026 by Zeenat, a passionate Ghanaian writer and lover of African oral tradition, our platform was born from a simple idea: to keep our grandmothers' stories alive in the digital age.

Our Story

Growing up in Buipe, I listened to stories under moonlight — stories of the river bride, the wise tortoise, the brave hunter, and lessons about love, family, and respect. Today, many of these stories are disappearing. ZEENATFLIX GH was created to preserve them and share them with Ghanaians at home and in the diaspora, and with the world.

What We Share

We publish 100% original stories written by our team:

  • African Folktales & Ananse Stories
  • Ghanaian Love Stories & Family Drama
  • Mystery, Suspense and Moral Lessons
  • Life in Savannah Region - Culture and Traditions

Why You Can Trust Us

Every story on ZEENATFLIX GH is written and edited by a human writer from Ghana. We do not copy stories from other websites. We research our culture, talk to elders, and add our own life lessons. Our content is family-friendly and suitable for all ages.

Our Mission

To become Ghana's #1 digital library for African stories that entertain, educate, and inspire the next generation, while creating opportunities for Ghanaian writers.

Meet the Founder

Zeenat is the Founder and Lead Writer of ZEENATFLIX GH, based in Buipe. She is a Ghanaian storyteller with a passion for preserving Savannah culture through modern writing. When she is not writing, she is teaching and engaging with readers.
